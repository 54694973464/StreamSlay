FAQ

Quick answers

What is StreamLive?
−
StreamLive is an all-in-one streaming platform giving you 10,000+ live TV channels, 50,000+ radio stations, music, movies, and on-demand content — all from one beautiful app on any device.
Is there a free plan?
+
Yes — StreamLive offers a free tier with 500+ channels and 5,000+ radio stations forever, with no credit card required. Upgrade to Pro or Premium for the full experience.
How many devices can I use?
+
Pro allows up to 3 simultaneous streams, and Premium allows up to 5 with full family sharing and individual profiles for each household member.
Can I cancel at any time?
+
Absolutely. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel from your account settings at any time — your subscription remains active until the end of your billing period.
What streaming quality is available?
+
StreamLive supports SD, HD, and 4K Ultra HD. Our adaptive bitrate engine automatically optimises quality based on your internet connection speed in real time.
